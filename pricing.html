Common questions
Is the AI assistant real legal advice? ↓
No. The AI provides general legal information based on UK law — the same as our free guides, but personalised to your situation. It is not advice from a qualified solicitor. For complex or high-value matters, we recommend consulting a solicitor at solicitors.lawsociety.org.uk.
Can I cancel anytime? ↓
Yes. Cancel through your PayPal account at any time. No contracts, no fees. Your access continues until the end of your billing period.
What areas of law does the AI cover? ↓
Currently: UK consumer rights (Consumer Rights Act 2015, refunds, faulty goods, scams) and online/social media rights (UK GDPR, Online Safety Act 2023, content removal, harassment). Employment and housing rights are coming soon.
What payment methods do you accept? ↓
We use PayPal, which accepts all major debit and credit cards, bank accounts, and PayPal balances. We never see your card details — PayPal handles everything securely.
Is there a free trial? ↓
The free guides and tools (letter builder, rights checker, SAR generator) don't require an account. For a trial of the AI assistant, email hello@knowyourrights.co.uk and we'll set one up.
